KILLKAIYOPERSONAL STATEMENT · MARCELINA PAGE
A personal account

Speaking about the harassment and threats I say I have experienced

Marcelina Page describes an ongoing pattern of online and in-person intimidation, the evidence she says she has preserved, and the steps she is taking to seek help and legal protection.

By Marcelina Page / Марселина Паж  ·  Pennsylvania  ·  October 2026
Editorial and factual note: This is a first-person account based on statements Marcelina has made in conversations. It is not independent journalism, an investigation, or a finding of fact. Allegations below are attributed to her and have not been independently verified here. No individual is named or accused as having committed a crime.

I am publishing this statement to document, in my own words, what I say I have been experiencing and why I am pursuing formal avenues for protection. I describe the situation as an ongoing pattern of harassment, intimidation, threats, and reputational harm that has affected my sense of safety and my ability to participate freely in online communities.

What I say has been happening

I have described the people involved as including former friends and acquaintances, as well as former domestic partners. I allege that some individuals have targeted me directly and publicly, communicated with one another about me, and engaged in conduct that I believe is coordinated. I have also described explicit threats of physical violence and statements that I understood as attempts to frighten or pressure me.

I have said that threats and intimidation have extended beyond online interactions. In particular, I have reported threats involving visits to homes and alleged incidents in which people were sent to my home and to the homes of family members. I understood this conduct as an effort to intimidate, harass, or cause harm to me and people close to me. These are serious allegations and should be assessed by the appropriate authorities rather than treated as established conclusions.

I have also described being told to leave my city. In messages I previously discussed, the phrases included “Like die” and “Literally plz leave my city.” I said the harassment had continued for weeks and that the people involved knew I was considering legal action. I have expressed concern that the timing and context were intended to intimidate me. Whether any particular message meets a legal definition is a matter for a court or relevant authority.

Evidence I say I have preserved

I have stated that I possess screenshots and copies of messages that I believe document threats, harassment, and statements made about me. I have also referred to other digital material and recordings in prior discussions. I have not made those materials available for independent review as part of this page, so this statement does not claim that they prove any particular allegation.

Where evidence exists, it should be preserved in its original form, with dates, account identifiers, and surrounding context intact, and provided through appropriate legal or investigative channels. Any claims about a person’s identity, intent, admissions, or legal responsibility require careful authentication and independent assessment.

Seeking protection and reporting concerns

I have said that I live in Pennsylvania and am preparing paperwork to seek an order of protection against multiple individuals. I have also discussed pursuing a police report and a civil lawsuit. At the time of the statements summarized here, I had described myself as preparing or pursuing these steps; this page does not represent that an order has already been granted or that a lawsuit has been filed or decided.

I have also said that I made reports to the FBI’s Counterterrorism program and to multiple Philadelphia-area agencies or police districts. I do not know whether those reports resulted in an investigation, and this page does not imply that any agency has validated my allegations, opened a case, or reached a conclusion.

Personal impact

I have described the experience as deeply distressing and traumatic, and said that it has affected my emotional well-being and sense of security. I have also described myself as a transgender woman and a disabled person, and have expressed the belief that hostility toward those aspects of my identity may be part of the context. That is my account of the situation; the motives of other people have not been independently established here.

My purpose in speaking publicly is to explain why I am documenting these events, asking for help, and seeking appropriate legal protections. I want any concerns to be evaluated through evidence, due process, and the proper channels—not through speculation, retaliation, or online escalation.
